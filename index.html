About Scrum Release Notes

Your company uses Jira Atlassian every day to manage software development with the Agile Scrum process.You are an executive, a product manager, a scrum master or anyone that wants to stay up to date with your projects progress on the go with a simple UI. This app is for you.

Look at all your projects closed sprints, burndown chart, team velocity, search for specific issues and add comments.

The Scrum Release Notes app is the easy way to stay inform on how your Scrum projects are doing.

App Features
Project Velocity

Measure how much work is getting done on your project over sprints

Developers breakdown

Have a sense of what each individual contribute to the project per sprint.

Add comments

Read, search stories & bugs, add comments when things are not clear.
